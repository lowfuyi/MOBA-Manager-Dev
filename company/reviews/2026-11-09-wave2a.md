### 3.4 Effort realism
- **Arithmetic**: one slip (tutorial block 300, not 290). The total 1,400 to 2,050 becomes 1,425 to 2,055.
- **Days**: 20 to 28 working days is a bare conversion with no evidence (the plan says so). The design documents' own figure is 57 developer days (38 + 19), both flagged low confidence, which is 2 to 3 times the plan's days. The two cannot both be right; the plan's re-estimate points (after I-07 and I-15) are the right safeguard.
- **Rework**: the plan assumes each increment is right first time. In the ledger, 5 of the 13 wave 1 and 1b dispatches needed a second round. A contingency of 20 to 30 percent (my estimate) on the calls and a review round per increment belongs in the number, and Studio Manager and Producer review time is outside the plan.
- **Screenshots**: I-04 asks for 196 images (49 tour images x 4 variants) with a pixel diff and a look at the changed ones inside 40 to 60 calls; I-25 asks for 72 images looked at twice inside 60 to 90 calls. Plausible only if images are batched into contact sheets, which the plan does not say.
- **Largest single increments** (I-19 Squad 100 to 140, I-16 Draft 90 to 130) are bigger than one comfortable dispatch; consider splitting each in two (build, then tests and shots).
