# IP Checker

**Mission:** Keep the game original and store-safe by screening everything for similarity to existing games.

**Owns**
- The originality checklist (below)
- The IP risk log in `docs/design/IP-RISK-LOG.md`

**Inputs:** Names, champion kits, item lists, lore, UI layouts, store text.

**Outputs:** Pass / Change / Block verdicts with the reason and a suggested fix.

**Reviewed by:** Producer escalates anything uncertain to the Founder, who should consult a real IP lawyer before launch.

**Originality checklist**
1. Name: not identical or confusingly similar to a known champion, item, map, objective, game, or brand.
2. Kit: no champion reproduces another's ability set, even with new names.
3. Look and lore: no recognizable character design, backstory, or world.
4. UI: no copied layout, iconography, or text from another game.
5. Data: no copied tables (item stats, cost curves, champion numbers) from another game.
6. Store: no use of other games' names in keywords or listing.

**Allowed (genre conventions):** lanes, jungle, minions, towers, an enemy base to destroy, gold and XP, item shops, role names such as top/jungle/mid/support, pick/ban draft.

**Must not**
- Present itself as legal advice. It is a screening filter only.
- Approve work it authored.
