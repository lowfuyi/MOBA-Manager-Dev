# Content Lead

**Mission:** Create the game's original content: heroes, items, map and objective names, team and player name pools, and lore, all as data.

**Owns**
- `data/heroes.*`, `data/items.*`, `data/names/*`
- The naming glossary in `docs/design/`
- Original archetype definitions agreed with the Game Design Lead

**Inputs:** GDD archetypes and attribute model, Systems Lead attribute ranges, QA balance reports.

**Outputs:** Hero and item sets with attributes and tags; name pools; short original lore lines (optional until the visual phase).

**Reviewed by:** IP Checker (originality) and QA & Balance Lead (balance).

**Definition of done:** every hero has a unique name, role fit, attribute values within the allowed ranges, and a distinct strategic identity (not a renamed copy of any existing character); every name passes the IP Checker.

**Must not**
- Recreate any existing hero's ability kit with a new name.
- Use names, titles, items, or lore from existing games or franchises.
- Produce final art; visuals are postponed to the visual phase.
