# Monetization draft (POSTPONED until the final release)

Status: concept only. Nothing here is built in Phase 1. Owner: Game Design Lead. Reviewer: IP Checker, then Founder.

## Founder direction (2026-11-07)
- Three subscription tiers: **Basic**, **Pro+**, **Esports**, each monthly or annual.
- Our own version, not a copy of the reference game.
- Decided and built only near the final release.

## What the reference screenshots show (observation only)
Three tiers with monthly/yearly toggle, a yearly discount ("2 months free"), a free trial on the middle tier, and a top tier that is mostly access and recognition. Observed price points: low tier about $2.99/month ($31.98/year), middle tier about $8.33/month billed yearly ($99.90/year), top tier $25.99/month. These are market reference points for planning, not our prices.

We do **not** reuse its tier names, wording, perk list or layout. Our tier names are the Founder's (Basic, Pro+, Esports), which are generic.

## Principles
1. **No paid advantage in match results.** Subscriptions sell convenience, customization and support, never stronger players or better odds. (Protects the "skill beats spending" pillar and store policy.)
2. **Free tier is a complete game.** Single Player must be fully playable and fair without paying.
3. **Perks must exist in the edition.** The offline Single Player edition has no servers, so its perks are about convenience and personalization. Online perks arrive with the Multiplayer and Global editions.
4. **Plain pricing.** Price, billing period and renewal shown clearly; trial terms stated; easy cancel (store rules require it).
5. **Billing through the store.** Android subscriptions use Google Play Billing. Policy check before building.

## Draft tier sketch (to be revised near release)

| Tier | Idea | Single Player edition perks (draft) | Added with Multiplayer / Global (draft) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | Support the game | No ads (if any ads exist), supporter badge | Supporter role in the community space |
| Pro+ | Convenience and customization | Everything in Basic, plus: custom club logo and colors, rename players, strategy presets and folders, extra draft automation options, historical stats and comparison tools, skip progress reports, bigger save/scouting quotas | Compare non-owned players, special notifications |
| Esports | Top supporter | Everything in Pro+, plus: club tag styling, a vote in the roadmap, early access to test builds | Direct line to the developer, priority matchmaking features that do not change match odds |

Notes:
- Basic Quick-mode auto-draft stays free, because Pillar 5 (short sessions) needs it. Paid automation means extra options (presets, rules), not the basic feature.
- Prices are not set. Planning range from the reference: low tier a few dollars, middle tier under ten, top tier a premium price. Singapore and other regional pricing to be decided with Play Console price templates.
- Free trial and an annual discount are likely, since the reference shows both work for the middle tier.
