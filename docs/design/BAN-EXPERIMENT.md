# Ban experiment: Classic against Standard (after the 0.11.1 balance pass)

Founder decision of 2026-11-22 (DECISIONS.md, B). Generated by `tools/ban_experiment.py` from `game/tests/ban_exp.tscn`: 40,000 games per format, real AI-vs-AI drafts in each format's order between clubs of the default world (a new patch every 40 games), then a Match Sim v2 game. The two teams keep everything their draft gave them (composition, style, lane counters, kits, items) and their ratings are moved, by one constant per team, to means of 50 + gap and 50, so the GDD 9 rating bands can be read with real drafts. Gap 0 is a third of all games (side balance, hero win rates). Run time 137 minutes on 7 desktop processes. Nothing here is measured on a phone.

## 0.11.1 balance pass: before and after (Producer, 2026-11-23)

The Founder approved both proposals of the 0.11.0 report (2026-11-22): about 5% more weight on rating, and a hero balance pass from real-draft data. Both are in 0.11.1. This page is the same 40,000-games-per-format experiment re-run on the 0.11.1 data. The 0.11.0 report is kept below under "Before".

**What changed**

- Rating weight `q_k` 0.36 to 0.378 (+5%) in `sim_v2_params.json`.
- Per-hero numbers in `hero_balance_v2.json`, tuned by `tools/tune_heroes_real.py`. Each round plays 8,000 equal-team games per format (1, 3 and 5 bans, real AI drafts) and moves only heroes outside 47.5 to 52.5, by at most 12% a round. `scale` multiplies damage, healing, shields and half of health. Two optional entries are new in 0.11.1 (`SimData.balance_dmg`, `balance_hp`):
  - **`dmg`** pins the damage of 8 heroes at their 0.11.0 value: the supports that were raised (Alderwyn, Fennimore, Sunnivah, Tanglewick, Wyncote) and the carries that were lowered (Harkin, Pellam, Dovrin).
  - **`hp`** pins the health of the 4 healing and shielding supports (Alderwyn, Sunnivah, Tanglewick, Wyncote).
  - Why: a plain scale moved kills from carries to supports. The share of kills that carries get fell from 1.63 to 1.23 times the supports' share. With the pins, a support's buff goes into its own job (healing and shields), and a carry's nerf goes into its health. Carries now get 1.48 times the supports' kills (240 games). Both pins keep every hero's role and kit shape.
- **Fennimore** (support, a crowd-control hero with little damage and no healing) changed in its kit, in `tools/gen_kits.py`:
  - Bog Bind lands 85% of the time instead of 75% and roots twice as long;
  - Croak slows on every second ability instead of every third;
  - Fen Fog is 25% wider and lasts 3.5 s instead of 3.0 s.
  - Health is x1.38 (scale 1.759; damage pinned at 1.274).
- **Harkin** (bot carry): Whaler's Mark (the long shot) lands 60% of the time instead of 70%. Health x0.76 (scale 0.523; damage pinned at 0.959).
- 9 heroes are unchanged, and the median scale change is 9%. The large scale moves are mostly heal and shield power on pinned supports: Alderwyn +52%, Sunnivah +33%, Tanglewick +27%, Wyncote +25%. Other large moves: Harkin -45% (health only), Hegwin -22%, Barrowick -21%, Lanthorn +21%, Dusken +20%, Brannoch +19%. No hero changed role, archetype or abilities.

**Rating rows at the line.** 1 ban +10 reads 66.96 against a floor of 67, which is 0.08 standard errors under (6,700 games, standard error 0.57). 5 bans +30 reads 89.89 against 90, which is 0.3 standard errors under (6,600 games, standard error 0.37). Both are inside the ±2 standard errors the calibration of record allows, so they count as passing. All 15 rows sit in the lower half of their bands, though. If the Founder wants them centred, the next step is `q_k` +7% (0.385) instead of +5%. That needs his approval, because +5% is what he approved.

**The one miss: Fennimore.** 42.5 / 43.7 / 45.8% (1 / 3 / 5 bans; about 4,300 games each, standard error 0.75), up from 38.9 / 40.8 / 41.3%. Nothing the numbers can do moves it further:
- health +38% and three crowd-control buffs together gave about +4 points;
- raising its damage to 1.35 moved it only 0.3 points, and it pushed the carry/support kill share under its line.

In this simulation a support that only roots and slows, with no healing or shields, is weak. **Proposal (Founder decision):** give Fennimore a small support effect that fits the frog: Fen Fog also heals allies inside it a little, or Leap Frog shields. That changes its kit a little more than numbers do, so it waits for his yes. Until then Fennimore is a weak pick, and the draft AI already picks it less.

**Not changed:**
- the draft AI and the ban orders;
- the role floor and comfort-hero findings (sections 4 and 5 are the same as before; the drafts do not depend on hero strength);
- the synthetic calibration of record (`MATCH-SIM-V2-CALIBRATION.md`, random picks). It was not re-run, so with the new rating weight its bands may now read slightly high.

The golden vectors were re-recorded on purpose (DECISIONS 0.11.1).

### Findings and proposals (Producer, 2026-11-22)

**Short answer.** Going from 1 to 3 to 5 bans per side changes almost nothing that decides games. The rating bands, game length, kills and the win rate of a team whose comfort heroes were banned stay within about 1 point across the three formats. What does change is the draft itself: more heroes are contested (presence over 50% for 1, 11, then 20 heroes), and each team plays heroes it is less familiar with. That was the point of the change. No calibration constant was changed for this report.

1. **Bands +10, +20 and +30 read 1 to 2 points under their bands in every format, Classic included.** Bans are not the cause: the 1-ban column is the draft the game already ships, and it reads the same. The cause is how this harness measures. Real drafts give each team a composition, style and lane counters that do not depend on the rating gap, and that noise pulls every row toward 50. The calibration of record (`MATCH-SIM-V2-CALIBRATION.md`) uses random role-correct picks and has every row in band. **Proposal:** keep the constants. If the Founder wants the bands to hold with real drafts too, the Systems Designer can raise the rating weight by about 5% and re-run both calibrations. That would be a deliberate change, with the golden vectors regenerated. Waiting for the Founder's word.
2. **Hero win rates at equal teams: 13 to 15 of 40 heroes are outside 45 to 55, in every format.** The worst is 38.8% (Alderwyn, Classic). With about 2,000 or more games per hero the standard error is about 1.1 points, so this is real and not noise. It existed before this change: Classic has 15 heroes out, 5 bans has 13. The calibration of record had 0 out, but it used random picks. Real drafts put heroes in the compositions and with the players the AI prefers. **Proposal:** a hero balance pass on `hero_balance_v2.json`, run by the Balance Designer from real-draft data (this harness). Held as a separate increment for the Founder's approval, because it changes results and the golden vectors.
3. **Side balance.** Blue wins 51.0% at equal teams with 5 bans (13,400 games, standard error 0.43). That is inside the 47 to 53 band, but 2.3 standard errors above 50. The 5-ban order gives Blue picks 1, 4, 5, 8 and 9, and Red 2, 3, 6, 7 and 10. Red bans first in phase 2 and has the last pick, to balance Blue's first pick. Kept as designed. If the 0.7 point becomes a problem, the lever is letting Red ban first in phase 1 as well.
4. **Comfort heroes and AI bans.** With 5 bans a team loses 2.81 of its 5 comfort heroes on average (Blue 2.81, Red 2.81), and 63% of teams lose 3 or more. That sounds harsh, but a team that lost 2 or more still wins 49.9% at equal teams (25,241 games). The bans spread across the team and do not decide the game. The familiarity of the heroes actually played falls from about 61 to about 54, which is the intended cost of a deeper draft. **No AI ban-spread rule is needed.** Both sides are hit the same, and losing comfort heroes does not change the result.
5. **Role floor.** 100,000 random 5-ban drafts gave no dead ends. The GDD 6 floor would have stepped in on 3.6% of random drafts and on 711 AI moves in 40,000 games. Off-role picks are always allowed, so a team always fills five lanes.
6. **Human draft time (not measured on a person).** A 5-ban draft asks the player for 10 decisions: 5 bans and 5 picks. The worst case is 10 × 15 s (every timer runs out), plus 10 AI turns of 0.9 s, about 2 min 39 s. A player who confirms the Coach suggestion each time needs about 2 to 3 s a decision, so about 40 s in total. A 3-ban draft is 8 decisions: 2 min 7 s worst case. The Classic league draft is 4 decisions × 10 s. **Timers kept at 15 s.** The timer is a ceiling, not a pace, and a 10-second ban timer would rush first-time players on the screen that is new to them. The Founder's phone test is the real measurement.
7. **Cost.** An AI draft takes 26 to 29 ms on desktop in every format, so more bans cost nothing. Not measured on a phone.
