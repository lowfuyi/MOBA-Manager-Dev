# Ban experiment: Classic against Standard

Founder decision of 2026-11-22 (DECISIONS.md, B). Generated by `tools/ban_experiment.py` from `game/tests/ban_exp.tscn`: 40,000 games per format, real AI-vs-AI drafts in each format's order between clubs of the default world (a new patch every 40 games), then a Match Sim v2 game. The two teams keep everything their draft gave them (composition, style, lane counters, kits, items) and their ratings are moved, by one constant per team, to means of 50 + gap and 50, so the GDD 9 rating bands can be read with real drafts. Gap 0 is a third of all games (side balance, hero win rates). Run time 161 minutes on 5 desktop processes. Nothing here is measured on a phone.

## Findings and proposals (Producer, 2026-11-22)

**Short answer.** Going from 1 to 3 to 5 bans per side changes almost nothing that decides games. The rating bands, game length, kills and the win rate of a team whose comfort heroes were banned stay within about 1 point across the three formats. What does change is the draft itself: more heroes are contested (presence over 50% for 1, 11, then 20 heroes), and each team plays heroes it is less familiar with. That was the point of the change. No calibration constant was changed for this report.

1. **Bands +10, +20 and +30 read 1 to 2 points under their bands in every format, Classic included.** Bans are not the cause: the 1-ban column is the draft the game already ships, and it reads the same. The cause is how this harness measures. Real drafts give each team a composition, style and lane counters that do not depend on the rating gap, and that noise pulls every row toward 50. The calibration of record (`MATCH-SIM-V2-CALIBRATION.md`) uses random role-correct picks and has every row in band. **Proposal:** keep the constants. If the Founder wants the bands to hold with real drafts too, the Systems Designer can raise the rating weight by about 5% and re-run both calibrations. That would be a deliberate change, with the golden vectors regenerated. Waiting for the Founder's word.
2. **Hero win rates at equal teams: 13 to 15 of 40 heroes are outside 45 to 55, in every format.** The worst is 38.8% (Alderwyn, Classic). With about 2,000 or more games per hero the standard error is about 1.1 points, so this is real and not noise. It existed before this change: Classic has 15 heroes out, 5 bans has 13. The calibration of record had 0 out, but it used random picks. Real drafts put heroes in the compositions and with the players the AI prefers. **Proposal:** a hero balance pass on `hero_balance_v2.json`, run by the Balance Designer from real-draft data (this harness). Held as a separate increment for the Founder's approval, because it changes results and the golden vectors.
3. **Side balance.** Blue wins 51.0% at equal teams with 5 bans (13,400 games, standard error 0.43). That is inside the 47 to 53 band, but 2.3 standard errors above 50. The 5-ban order gives Blue picks 1, 4, 5, 8 and 9, and Red 2, 3, 6, 7 and 10. Red bans first in phase 2 and has the last pick, to balance Blue's first pick. Kept as designed. If the 0.7 point becomes a problem, the lever is letting Red ban first in phase 1 as well.
4. **Comfort heroes and AI bans.** With 5 bans a team loses 2.81 of its 5 comfort heroes on average (Blue 2.81, Red 2.81), and 63% of teams lose 3 or more. That sounds harsh, but a team that lost 2 or more still wins 49.9% at equal teams (25,241 games). The bans spread across the team and do not decide the game. The familiarity of the heroes actually played falls from about 61 to about 54, which is the intended cost of a deeper draft. **No AI ban-spread rule is needed.** Both sides are hit the same, and losing comfort heroes does not change the result.
5. **Role floor.** 100,000 random 5-ban drafts gave no dead ends. The GDD 6 floor would have stepped in on 3.6% of random drafts and on 711 AI moves in 40,000 games. Off-role picks are always allowed, so a team always fills five lanes.
6. **Human draft time (not measured on a person).** A 5-ban draft asks the player for 10 decisions: 5 bans and 5 picks. The worst case is 10 × 15 s (every timer runs out), plus 10 AI turns of 0.9 s, about 2 min 39 s. A player who confirms the Coach suggestion each time needs about 2 to 3 s a decision, so about 40 s in total. A 3-ban draft is 8 decisions: 2 min 7 s worst case. The Classic league draft is 4 decisions × 10 s. **Timers kept at 15 s.** The timer is a ceiling, not a pace, and a 10-second ban timer would rush first-time players on the screen that is new to them. The Founder's phone test is the real measurement.
7. **Cost.** An AI draft takes 26 to 29 ms on desktop in every format, so more bans cost nothing. Not measured on a phone.

## 5. Role floor (GDD 6)

Random drafts (100,000 per format, any available hero, any open lane) and the AI's drafts above. A dead end is a team unable to fill five lanes; a bind is a moment where the GDD 6 floor (after a pick, as many primary-role heroes left as teams still needing the slot; after a ban, one more) would have blocked the move. Off-role picks are always allowed, so the floor is a safety net.
