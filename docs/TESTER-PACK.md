# Gloamline Alpha: tester pack

Three short parts for people who are not technical: how to install the game, how to send feedback, and what to try.
The Founder sends this page, the APK file and the feedback form link together.

---

## Part 1. How to install (Android only, about 3 minutes)

Gloamline is not in the Play Store yet. You install it from a file, which Android calls an "APK".

1. **Get the file onto your phone.** Open the message or email with the file `gloamline-...-alpha.apk` on your phone and tap
   it to download it. (If you got it on a computer, send it to yourself by email or chat and open it on the phone.)
2. **Open the file.** Tap the download notification, or open the **Files** (or **Downloads**) app and tap the file.
3. **Allow the install.** Android will say something like "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps
   from this source". Tap **Settings**, switch on **Allow from this source**, then go back. This permission is only for the app
   you downloaded the file with (for example Chrome or Gmail). You can switch it off again afterwards.
4. **Install.** Tap **Install**. If Google Play Protect warns that it does not know the app, tap **More details**, then
   **Install anyway**. (It warns because the game is a test build that is not in the store, not because anything was found.)
5. **Open it.** The game is called **Gloamline Alpha** on your home screen.

**Updating:** when you get a newer file, install it the same way. It installs over the old one and **keeps your save**. Do not
uninstall first, or your save is lost.

**If it will not install:** "App not installed" usually means not enough free space, or an older copy signed differently. Tell
the Founder the exact message and your phone model.

**Privacy:** nothing leaves your phone. The game has no internet permission, no account and no ads.

## Part 2. How to send feedback (about 5 minutes)

1. In the game, tap **More** (the last tab, bottom right) to open Settings, scroll to **Send feedback** and tap
   **Copy feedback note**. This copies a short note with the game version, how far you got, your settings and how long games
   took. It contains no personal information.
2. Open the **feedback form link** the Founder sent you. Paste the note into the question that asks for it (press and hold in the
   box, then **Paste**).
3. Answer what you can. Only a few questions are required. The most useful answers are the honest ones: "I did not understand
   X" helps more than a polite score.
4. **Found a bug or a crash?** In the form's "Problems" section, say what you tapped just before, what you expected and what
   happened. A screenshot helps (press power and volume-down together), sent to the Founder by chat.

You can send the form more than once, for example once after your first evening and once after a full season.

## Part 3. What to test (one page)

Play the way you normally would. Do not read the rest of this first if you want to be a true first-time player; come back to it
after your first game.

**First 15 minutes**
- Start a new game and follow the guide. Did you ever feel lost? Where?
- Finish your first match. Did you understand why you won or lost from the result screen?

**Watching a match**
- Watch at least three matches without skipping. Fights now show heroes charging, shooting and healing, health bars and the
  deciding kill. Do fights look like fights? Can you tell who is winning a fight before someone falls?
- Try **Speed x2** once. Too fast to follow?
- More (Settings) > **Frame rate**: try 30 and 60 for a few matches each. Does 30 look choppy? Does the phone feel cooler or the
  battery last longer at 30?
- More (Settings) > **Sound and vibration**: is the sound pleasant, annoying, or something you switch off at once?

**A full season** (about 30 to 45 minutes over a few sittings)
- Play one whole season: draft, Game Plan, training, contracts, finance, standings, playoffs.
- Does anything feel pointless, confusing or repetitive? After how many matches did it start to feel the same?
- Did the game ever crash, freeze, or lose your save?

**Comfort**
- More (Settings) > Comfort: try **Larger text** and the **colour-blind friendly** colours. Is everything still readable?

**The one question that matters most:** would you play this again next week without being asked? Why, or why not?

Thank you. Every answer goes into the next build.
